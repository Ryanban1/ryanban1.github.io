## Problem
Belongings left unattended can be stolen. Our team set out to build a Raspberry Pi device that protects them: it watches the belongings and alerts the owner's phone when someone approaches or takes an item.

## My role
I led a team of five through the build and directed the team's CAD design in SolidWorks. We validated demand through online surveys and interviews, then pitched the product as a startup venture. In the program's labs I also built and programmed Arduino circuits in C++.

## How it works
- A camera on a Raspberry Pi watches the belongings, and OpenCV detects when a person approaches and whether an item is taken.
- On a detection, the device sends an alert to the owner's phone and triggers an alarm and a flashing light.
- The owner can open a live camera feed from their phone at any time.

## Tools
Raspberry Pi, camera, OpenCV, SolidWorks.

## Results
- Built a Raspberry Pi device that detects when someone approaches or takes an item, alerts the owner's phone and sounds an alarm
