## Problem
When a rider crashes, parents may not find out where or when it happened. I wanted a helmet that could tell them right away.

## My role
I conceived the helmet and produced its design drawings, then validated the concept through surveys and interviews.

## How it works
The helmet uses AI to detect a crash and sends parents an alert with the rider's GPS location.

## Results
- Pitched the concept to venture capitalists and UC Berkeley professors
- Selected as 1 of 50 students for the Berkeley Business Academy for Youth
